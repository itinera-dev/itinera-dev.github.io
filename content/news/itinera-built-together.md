# The story of how Itinera was built

[Itinera built together]({{series}}) is a series of fourteen articles by Itinera's maintainer, Marlon Sousa, on his own site. It tells how Itinera was made: the evening it was first thought through, the spec-first process designed with an agent and then held to, agents reviewing what other agents proposed, and the decisions that stayed with a person.

The conversations with the agents are quoted on both sides, exactly as they happened. It is the long version of [how Itinera is built with AI](/contribute/#how-itinera-is-built-with-ai).

## Start reading

[The series]({{series}}) opens with how long Itinera would have taken without agents, and how long it took with them. Every article stands on its own, and each one after the first opens with a summary of the one before it.
